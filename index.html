Update Ease

Edit rotating submenus

Add the ideas you want Ease to rotate beneath Write, Yard, Home, and every other hourly tile. Use one option per line.
Each list rotates fairly. Individual options do not need their own cadence.

Edit Pinned

Keep a short sticky-note list of priorities that matter, even when they are not daily tasks.
Pinned items stay at the top of Ready. They do not become overdue and do not need a completion schedule.

Shape today

Tell Ease what belongs in today. Actionable items become Turns, and matching Tasks and recurrences move to the front.
Use commas, new lines, or short sentences. For example, “Home, writing, finances” will create today’s Turns and prioritize Tasks in those areas. Nothing becomes overdue or debt.

A home is not a deadline. Ease keeps this out of the active pool until the window arrives. If the time passes, it simply becomes available — no red badge, no debt.
